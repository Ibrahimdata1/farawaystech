# How to Choose a Thai Software House: 7 Things to Check

Choosing the wrong Software House can cost you **months of time and thousands of dollars** — projects that never finish, or finish but don't work. This guide covers **7 things to check** and **red flags to watch** before signing a contract.

---

## 1. Check Real Portfolio

The first thing to look at is **actual working projects**, not just pretty mockups.

**Must check:**
- Can you open and use the website/app? (Not just screenshots)
- Does it load fast? Is it easy to use?
- Do they have projects similar to yours?

> **Example:** See [RunAway Tech's portfolio](/#portfolio) — WyckoffSignals (data analytics dashboard) and QRForPay (restaurant QR menu system).

---

## 2. Check the Technology Stack

The technology they use affects **speed, security, and future maintenance costs**.

| Technology | Pros | Watch Out For |
|---|---|---|
| **WordPress** | Cheap, easy to use | Slow, constant plugin updates, easy to hack |
| **React / Next.js** | Very fast, great SEO, 100% customizable | Needs skilled developers |
| **PHP Laravel** | Good backend systems | Can be slower than Next.js |
| **No-code (Wix, Bubble)** | Fast, cheap | Limited, inflexible, you don't own the code |

> **Recommendation:** For business websites, choose a team using **React/Next.js** — fast, great SEO, easy to maintain, no plugin update worries.

---

## 3. Ask About Post-Launch Support

**This is critical** — 90% of problems happen after delivery, not during development.

**Questions to ask:**
- If the site breaks, how do I reach you? What's the response time?
- How long is the warranty period?
- What are monthly/yearly maintenance costs?
- How are additional features billed later?

---

## 4. Evaluate Communication

Good communication is as important as coding skills.

**Good signs:**
- Responds quickly (within 24 hours)
- Explains technical concepts in simple language
- Asks detailed questions before quoting
- Provides regular progress updates

**Bad signs:**
- Takes 3-5 days to reply
- Speaks only in technical jargon you can't understand
- Quotes immediately without asking for details

---

## 5. Understand the Pricing Structure

| Model | Pros | Cons | Best For |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Fixed Price** | Know exact cost upfront | Scope changes cost extra | Projects with clear scope |
| **Time & Material** | Flexible, adjustable | Cost is uncertain | Projects where scope may change |
| **Retainer** (monthly) | Continuous support | Ongoing monthly cost | Systems needing continuous development |

## 6. Ask About Ownership

**Most important of all** — after paying, you must own:

- **Source code** — All code is 100% yours
- **Domain** — Your website name belongs to you
- **Hosting access** — You can access the server yourself
- **Data** — All data belongs to you

> **Warning:** Some Software Houses don't hand over source code — if you stop working with them, you have to rebuild everything from scratch. Ask clearly before signing any contract.

---

## 7. Check Reviews and References

- Look at Google Reviews, Facebook Reviews
- Can you talk to past clients? (Good Software Houses will happily provide references)
- Look for published articles/work — demonstrates expertise

---

## Red Flags to Watch Out For

| Red Flag | Why It's Dangerous |
|---|---|
| **Price far below market rate** | Quality may be poor, or there are hidden costs |
| **They promise everything** | No Software House is expert at everything |
| **No portfolio** | No work to show = no experience |
| **Won't sign a contract** | No contract = no accountability |
| **Won't hand over source code** | You don't truly own what you paid for |
| **No clear timeline** | Project may drag on indefinitely |
| **Guarantees Google #1 ranking** | Nobody can guarantee SEO rankings |
