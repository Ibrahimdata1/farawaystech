# What is SEO? Why Every Business Needs SEO in 2026

If you have a shop with no sign outside, how would customers know you exist? **SEO is your sign on Google** — it helps people searching for your products or services find you first.

---

## What is SEO? Simple Explanation

**SEO (Search Engine Optimization)** is the process of making your website **rank at the top of Google** when people search for keywords related to your business.

Example: If you run an air conditioning repair shop in Bangkok, when someone searches "AC repair Bangkok" — SEO makes your website appear on page 1.

### SEO vs Paid Ads

| | SEO | Google Ads |
|--|-----|-----------|
| **Cost** | One-time or monthly | Pay per click |
| **Results** | Long-term (months-years) | Instant but disappears when you stop |
| **Credibility** | High (organic results) | Lower (people know it's an ad) |
| **Long-term ROI** | Very high | Low (must keep paying) |

> **Summary:** SEO is like planting a tree — takes time but gives free fruit every year. Ads are like buying fruit — instant but costs every time.

---

## Why Does Your Business Need SEO?

### 1. Customers Find You Without Paying for Ads
93% of customers start with a Google search before buying. If your website isn't on page 1, you're invisible to these customers.

### 2. Builds Credibility
Websites ranking organically on page 1 are perceived as more trustworthy than paid ads.

### 3. Long-term Results
Good SEO keeps working for months or years, unlike ads that disappear the moment you stop paying.

### 4. Reach Ready-to-Buy Customers
People searching on Google **actively need** your product/service — they're not just scrolling past like on Facebook.

---

## What Does SEO Involve?

### On-page SEO
- Optimize titles and descriptions with keywords
- Content structure (H1, H2, H3) that Google understands
- Quality content that answers customer questions

### Technical SEO
- Fast loading speed
- Sitemap for Google crawling
- Mobile-friendly design

### Off-page SEO
- Links from trusted websites
- Reviews and citations

---

## Which Businesses Should Do SEO?

**Every business** that wants customers from Google — especially:
- Restaurants/cafés ("restaurant near me")
- Clinics/spas ("dermatology clinic Bangkok")
- Retail ("buy [product] online")
- Service businesses ("repair/install [service]")
