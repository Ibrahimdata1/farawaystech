# Business Automation: Cut Costs and Increase Profit with Tech

Do you find yourself doing the same tasks every day? Summarizing sales, sending receipts, answering the same chat questions, reminding clients of appointments — **automation can handle all of this**.

---

## What is Business Automation?

Simply put: **letting technology do repetitive work for you**. Tasks that are routine, done daily, and don't require creativity — systems do them better, faster, without forgetting or making mistakes.

---

## 5 Tasks Every SME Should Automate

### 1. Customer Chat Responses
Customers ask the same questions daily — "What time do you open?" "How much?" "How do I book?" Use a chatbot to answer automatically.

### 2. Invoices and Receipts
Instead of typing receipts one by one, the system sends them automatically after payment.

### 3. Appointment Reminders
Auto LINE/SMS reminders 1 day before appointments. Reduce no-shows.

### 4. Daily Sales Reports
System generates sales summaries every morning. No manual calculations.

### 5. Inventory Management
When stock runs low, the system alerts you automatically.

---

## How Much Can You Save?

| Task | Manual | Automated | Monthly Savings |
|------|--------|-----------|----------------|
| Sales summary | 30 min/day | Instant | 15 hrs |
| Sending receipts | 10 min/order | Automatic | 20+ hrs |
| Reminders | Call each person | Auto SMS/LINE | 10+ hrs |
| Chat responses | All day | Chatbot | 15+ hrs |

> **Total: Save 60+ hours/month** — equivalent to one employee's salary
