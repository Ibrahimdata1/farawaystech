## Red Flags When Hiring an SEO Provider

### Warning Signs to Avoid

1. **"Guaranteed #1 ranking in 30 days"** — Nobody can guarantee that. Google changes its algorithm constantly.
2. **Won't explain what they do** — If they can't tell you what they're doing, they're probably doing very little.
3. **Uses Black Hat SEO** — Spammy tactics may work short-term but can get your site permanently banned.
4. **Long contracts with no reporting** — You should receive monthly reports showing what was done and the results.

### What Good SEO Providers Offer

- Monthly reports (keyword rankings, traffic, work performed)
- Clear explanations of strategy and tactics
- No spam techniques
- Portfolio of past results
