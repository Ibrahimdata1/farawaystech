# Your Own Online Store vs Selling on Shopee/Lazada

"I already sell on Shopee, why do I need my own website?" — this is one of the most common questions from online sellers. This article compares both options clearly so you can make **the best decision for your business**.

---

## Head-to-Head Comparison

| Factor | Shopee/Lazada Store | Your Own Website |
|---|---|---|
| **Commission fees** | 3-15% per order | **0%** |
| **Advertising costs** | Must pay to be visible | Free SEO + optional ads |
| **Customer data** | Shopee/Lazada keeps it | **100% yours** |
| **Branding** | Very limited, every store looks the same | **Full custom design** |
| **Customer loyalty** | Easy for customers to price-compare and switch | **Build your own customer base** |
| **Credibility** | Depends on platform | **Your own site looks professional** |
| **Rules** | Must follow platform rules | **You make your own rules** |
| **Startup cost** | Free (but commission on every sale) | One-time investment |

---

## Commission Fees: Money Lost Every Month

Assume your shop does **100,000 THB/month** (~$2,860) in sales on Shopee:

| Item | Amount/Month |
|---|---|
| Commission (average 8%) | 8,000 THB (~$229) |
| Payment processing fee (2%) | 2,000 THB (~$57) |
| Shopee Ads (minimum to compete) | 3,000 - 10,000 THB (~$86-$286) |
| **Total paid to platform** | **13,000 - 20,000 THB (~$371-$571)** |

**Per year: 156,000 - 240,000 THB ($4,460 - $6,860)** paid to Shopee.

> **Compare:** Building your own online store costs 15,000 - 35,000 THB one-time + hosting 2,000-5,000 THB/year. First year investment ~20,000-40,000 THB. **Year 2 onward: almost no costs.**

---

## Customer Data: The Most Valuable Thing You're Not Getting

When selling on Shopee/Lazada:

- You **don't get** customer emails
- You **don't get** customer phone numbers (only shipping numbers)
- You **can't** contact customers directly
- You **can't** do remarketing or email marketing

With your own website:
- Collect emails, phone numbers, addresses
- Send promotions directly to customers
- Run remarketing (Facebook Pixel, Google Ads)
- Build your own loyalty program

> **Customer data = assets.** The more you collect, the more valuable it becomes. If the data sits with the platform, you start from zero every day.

---

## Branding: Your Store vs Marketplace Store

| | Shopee/Lazada Store | Your Own Website |
|---|---|---|
| **Storefront** | Same template as every other store | Custom designed for your brand |
| **Customer experience** | Shopee controls it | You control it |
| **Logo/colors/fonts** | Very limited | Unlimited |
| **Will customers remember you?** | Hard — looks like everyone else | Easy — unique identity |

**Think about it:** The most successful brands all have their own websites. They don't rely solely on marketplaces.

---

## Which Should You Choose?

### Shopee/Lazada is best when:
- Just starting out, no established customer base
- Testing products before committing
- Zero investment budget
- Selling commodity products (competing on price)

### Your own website is best when:
- Building a long-term brand
- You want customer data
- You want to reduce commission costs
- Selling high-margin products
- Building customer loyalty

### The best strategy: **Do both.**

> **Recommended approach:** Use Shopee/Lazada as a **channel to acquire new customers**, then bring them to your **own website** for repeat purchases — get new customers from the marketplace and loyal customers from your site.

## 3-Year Cost Comparison

| | Shopee Only | Own Website + Shopee |
|---|---|---|
| **Year 1** | 156,000 - 240,000 THB (commission) | 40,000 THB (website) + 78,000-120,000 (Shopee at 50% reduced volume) |
| **Year 2** | 156,000 - 240,000 THB | 5,000 THB (hosting) + 39,000-60,000 (Shopee further reduced) |
| **Year 3** | 156,000 - 240,000 THB | 5,000 THB + website sales growing, less Shopee commission |
| **3-Year Total** | **468,000 - 720,000 THB** | **167,000 - 230,000 THB** |

> **Save 300,000 - 490,000 THB** (~$8,570 - $14,000) over 3 years by having your own online store.
