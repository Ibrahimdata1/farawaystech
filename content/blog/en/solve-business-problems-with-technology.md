## How Technology Solves Business Problems

### 1. Customers Can't Find You → Website + SEO

**Problem:** Customers search for products/services on Google but can't find your business. You rely entirely on paid ads that get more expensive every year.

**Solution:** Build a website designed to convert visitors into customers + SEO to rank on Google page 1.

**Results:**
- Customers find you **without paying for ads**
- Website works 24/7 like a sales employee
- Builds more credibility than a Facebook page alone

### 2. High Staff Costs → QR Ordering + POS System

**Problem:** Restaurants hire 2-3 staff just to take orders, paying 24,000-36,000 baht/month even during quiet hours.

**Solution:** QR ordering system — customers scan, order, and pay themselves. Combined with a POS for automatic sales reports.

**Results:**
- **Cut 1 staff = save 12,000 baht/month**
- Zero order errors (customers order themselves)
- Automatic daily sales reports

> Read more: [How a POS System Saves Restaurants Money](/blog/how-pos-system-saves-restaurant-money) | [See QRForPay](/#portfolio)

---

### 3. Booking Conflicts → Online Booking System

**Problem:** Clinics, spas, and salons spend all day answering phone calls for bookings. Appointments clash, notes get lost, no-shows go untracked.

**Solution:** Online booking through your website. Customers book 24/7 + automatic reminders before appointments.

**Results:**
- **80% less admin time** — no more phone booking
- Customers book anytime without calling
- Fewer no-shows with automated reminders

> Read more: [Online Booking Systems for Service Businesses](/blog/online-booking-system-service-business-2026)

---

### 4. Scattered Data → Business Analytics Dashboard

**Problem:** Sales data in Excel, customer info in LINE, inventory in a notebook — making decisions requires manually gathering data, which is slow and error-prone.

**Solution:** A dashboard that consolidates everything in one place, viewable in real-time from your phone.

**Results:**
- See today's sales instantly, don't wait for month-end
- Know which products sell well and which don't
- Make decisions based on data, not gut feeling

> Read more: [Data Dashboards: Secret Weapon for SMEs](/blog/data-dashboard-secret-weapon-sme) | [See WyckoffSignals](/#portfolio)

### 5. Inventory Mismatches → Full E-Commerce Platform

**Problem:** Selling on multiple channels (in-store, Shopee, LINE) but inventory doesn't sync. Customers order, but items are already sold out.

**Solution:** An online store that syncs inventory across all channels in real-time.

**Results:**
- Automatic inventory updates — no more overselling
- Manage orders from all channels in one place
- Customers can buy 24/7 through your website

> Read more: [Your Own Store vs Shopee/Lazada](/blog/own-online-store-vs-shopee-lazada)

### 6. Repetitive Daily Tasks → Business Automation

**Problem:** Staff doing the same tasks every day — sending receipts, summarizing sales, answering the same chat questions, reminding clients of appointments.

**Solution:** Automation systems that work 24/7, never tired, never forget, never make mistakes.

**Results:**
- **Save 10-20 hours/week** from manual work
- Staff can focus on more important tasks
- Fewer human errors

| Task | Manual | Automated | Monthly Savings |
|------|--------|-----------|----------------|
| Sales summary | 30 min/day | Instant | 15 hrs |
| Sending receipts | 10 min/order | Automatic | 20+ hrs |
| Appointment reminders | Call one by one | Auto SMS/LINE | 10+ hrs |
| Answering FAQs | All day | Chatbot | 15+ hrs |
