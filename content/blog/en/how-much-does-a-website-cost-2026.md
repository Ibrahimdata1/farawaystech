# How Much Does a Website Cost in 2026?

If you're thinking about building a website, the first question is always **"How much will it cost?"** — The answer is... **it depends.** But this guide gives you real market pricing data from both Thailand and international markets.

> **Exchange rates used:** 1 USD ≈ 35 THB | 1 GBP ≈ 45 THB (approximate)

---

## Website Pricing in Thailand

### 1. Landing Page / Single Page

> 🔹 **What is a Landing Page?**
>
> A **single-page** website that puts everything on one page — no clicking to other pages, just scroll down and you'll find everything: about the business, services, portfolio, reviews, and contact info.
>
> **Examples:** The [RunAwayTech](https://runawaytech.vercel.app) website is a Landing Page. Also: mobile app promo pages, event registration pages, online business cards.
>
> **Best for:** New businesses that want an online presence quickly without complexity.

| Provider | Price (THB) | Price (USD) |
|---|---|---|
| Freelancer (Fastwork) | 1,500 - 6,000 | ~$43 - $171 |
| Small company | 2,900 - 8,000 | ~$83 - $229 |

Best for: Freelancers, small shops, portfolios, new businesses — Timeline: 3-7 days

### 2. Business Website (5-10 pages)

> 🔹 **What is a Business Website?**
>
> A website with **multiple separate pages** — Home, About Us, Services, Blog, Contact — each with its own URL. Click the menu and you navigate to different pages.
>
> **How is it different from a Landing Page?** A Landing Page is one page you scroll through. A Business Website has many pages with more content, better organization, and looks more professional.
>
> **Examples:** A hotel website with separate pages for rooms, promotions, and bookings. A company site with team, portfolio, and articles pages.
>
> **Best for:** Companies that need credibility, lots of content, and good SEO.

| Provider | Price (THB) | Price (USD) |
|---|---|---|
| Template (WordPress) | 7,500 - 15,000 | ~$214 - $429 |
| Freelancer (custom) | 6,500 - 15,000 | ~$186 - $429 |
| Agency (custom design) | 15,000 - 35,000 | ~$429 - $1,000 |

Best for: SMEs, small to medium businesses that need credibility — Timeline: 1-3 weeks

### 3. E-Commerce Store

> 🔹 **What is an E-Commerce Store?**
>
> A website where customers can **actually buy products online** — browse products, add to cart, checkout, pay with credit card/bank transfer, and track their delivery.
>
> **How is it different from a Business Website?** A Business Website just displays information. An E-Commerce store has a real buying system inside — cart, payment processing, inventory management, receipts.
>
> **Examples:** Like your own Amazon or Shopee, but it's your store — no commission fees to a platform. Online clothing stores, supplement shops, etc.
>
> **Best for:** Shops that want their own online storefront without relying on marketplace platforms.

| Provider | Price (THB) | Price (USD) |
|---|---|---|
| Freelancer | 15,000 - 25,000 | ~$429 - $714 |
| Company (WooCommerce) | 22,500 - 35,000 | ~$643 - $1,000 |
| Agency (custom) | 35,000 - 80,000 | ~$1,000 - $2,286 |

Best for: Shops wanting their own online store without relying on Shopee/Lazada — Timeline: 2-6 weeks

### 4. Web Application / Custom System

> 🔹 **What is a Web Application?**
>
> **Not a regular website** — it's a **software program that runs in your browser**, like a mobile app but opened from a web browser. It has login systems, dashboards, data management, calculations, and processing.
>
> **How is it different from a regular website?** A regular website just "displays information" for people to read. A Web App actually does work — calculates, manages appointments, generates reports, processes payments, etc.
>
> **Examples:** Clinic booking systems (doctors see schedules, patients book online), restaurant POS (take orders, calculate bills, print receipts), CRM for managing customers, inventory management apps, HR systems.
>
> **Best for:** Businesses that need a specialized system that doesn't exist off-the-shelf — it has to be built from scratch.

| Provider | Price (THB) | Price (USD) |
|---|---|---|
| Freelancer (simple) | 50,000 - 150,000 | ~$1,429 - $4,286 |
| Agency | 150,000 - 500,000 | ~$4,286 - $14,286 |
| Software House (complex) | 500,000 - 2,000,000+ | ~$14,286 - $57,143+ |

Best for: Businesses needing specific solutions (POS, CRM, booking) — Timeline: 1-3+ months

**Thai developer rates:** Freelancers ~$9-23/hr | Agency staff ~$23-57/hr

## Can You Build a Website Yourself? (Wix, WordPress, Shopify)

Many people think "building it myself is cheaper" — and yes, **the price is lower**, but what you **trade off** is significant:

### The Downsides of DIY Websites

| Problem | Details |
|---------|---------|
| **Massive time investment** | Learning from scratch — choosing templates, drag-and-drop, configuring settings. Takes 2-4 weeks to get something usable. |
| **Unprofessional design** | Templates look the same as thousands of other sites. Limited customization. Customers can tell immediately it's DIY. |
| **Poor SEO** | Wix sites average 3.4s load time (optimized sites load in 1.2s). Bloated code, excessive HTTP requests, no deep SEO customization. Google ranks you lower. |
| **Stuck when things break** | When your site breaks or you need a custom feature, you can't fix it yourself — you end up hiring someone anyway. |
| **Hidden costs** | Looks cheap, but premium templates + plugins + hosting + domain = $150-600/year — close to hiring a professional. |
| **You don't truly own it** | Wix has no HTML/code export — stop paying and your site disappears. Shopify storefront goes offline after cancellation. (Self-hosted WordPress is the exception, but you manage your own server.) |
