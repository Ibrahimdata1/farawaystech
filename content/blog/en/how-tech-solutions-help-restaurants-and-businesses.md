# How Tech Solutions Help Restaurants and Businesses

If you run a restaurant, café, or SME — ask yourself:

- Still taking orders by hand?
- Counting inventory manually every day?
- Posting on Facebook and just waiting for customers?
- Customers call to reserve, and you write it in a notebook?
- Spending days at month-end totaling up sales from paper receipts?

If you answered "yes" to even one — this article will show you **how simple technology can save you time and money.**

---

## Common Problems Restaurants and SMEs Face

### 1. Wrong Orders, Angry Customers

Handwritten orders → illegible handwriting → kitchen makes the wrong dish → customer unhappy → lost regular customer.

> **Tech fix:** POS system or QR Code ordering — customers scan and order themselves, orders go straight to the kitchen. Zero mistakes, no extra staff needed.

### 2. No Idea What Sells and What Doesn't

Selling every day but never looking at which menu items are profitable and which are losing money.

> **Tech fix:** Sales dashboard showing daily/monthly revenue, top 10 best-sellers, profit per item — make decisions from real data, not gut feeling.

### 3. Stock Runs Out Without Warning

Ingredients run out during peak hours → must stop taking orders → lost revenue.

> **Tech fix:** Inventory management that auto-deducts when you sell, alerts when stock is low, so you reorder in time.

### 4. Customers Can't Find You on Google

You have a restaurant but searching on Google shows nothing — new customers have no way to discover you.

> **Tech fix:** A website with good SEO + Google Business Profile — customers search "restaurants near me" and find yours.

### 5. Managing Reservations with a Notebook

Customer calls to book → write in notebook → forget → double booking → customer arrives, no table.

> **Tech fix:** Online booking system — customers book through your website/LINE, see available tables, get automatic confirmation. No phone calls needed.
