# Data Dashboards: The Secret Weapon for SME Growth

Most SME owners make decisions based on gut feeling — "I think this product sells well" or "I feel like we're profitable." But what if you could **see actual numbers in real-time** from your phone?

That's what a business dashboard does. And it's more affordable than you think.

---

## What is a Business Dashboard?

A dashboard is a **visual display of your business data** — sales, customers, inventory, expenses — all in one screen. Think of it as the cockpit of your business: instead of guessing, you see exactly what's happening.

---

## Why Every SME Needs a Dashboard

### Know Your Actual Profit Every Day
No more waiting for month-end to find out if you're making money. See today's revenue, costs, and profit instantly.

### Spot Best-Sellers and Dead Stock
Know which products make money and which ones waste shelf space. Stop guessing, start optimizing.

### Make Decisions Based on Data
"Should I run a promotion?" "Should I hire more staff?" "Which branch performs better?" A dashboard gives you answers, not opinions.

---

## Dashboard Examples by Business Type

| Business | Key Metrics | Benefit |
|----------|------------|---------|
| Restaurant | Daily sales, top dishes, table turnover | Know which menu items to keep/remove |
| Retail | Inventory levels, sales by channel, margins | Never run out of popular items |
| Service | Bookings, revenue per service, no-show rate | Optimize scheduling and pricing |
