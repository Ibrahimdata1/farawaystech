# How a POS System Saves Restaurant Money: Real Calculations

If your restaurant still uses **paper order pads** or an **old-fashioned cash register**, you might be losing thousands of baht every month without realizing it. This article calculates exactly **how much a POS system can save you**.

---

## What is a POS System?

**POS (Point of Sale)** is a comprehensive restaurant/store management system that handles:

- **Order taking** — Staff tap on a tablet, or customers order via QR code
- **Billing** — Automatic calculation, accepts cash, card, and PromptPay
- **Inventory management** — Track ingredients, get alerts when running low
- **Reporting** — Daily/monthly sales, popular items, peak hours

---

## Problems Restaurants Face Without a POS System

| Problem | Impact | Money Lost/Month |
|---|---|---|
| **Wrong orders** (miswritten/illegible) | Remaking food, wasted ingredients, unhappy customers | 2,000 - 5,000 THB |
| **Billing errors** | Wrong change, forgetting to charge items | 1,000 - 3,000 THB |
| **Employee theft** | No receipt issued, cash pocketed | 3,000 - 10,000+ THB |
| **Inventory loss** | Ingredients run out unexpectedly, emergency purchases (more expensive) | 2,000 - 5,000 THB |
| **No sales data** | Don't know which dishes are profitable, which lose money | Cumulative losses without awareness |

**Total: A restaurant may lose 8,000 - 23,000+ THB/month from problems a POS system solves.**

---

## Real Calculation Example: Small Restaurant

Assume a restaurant with **150,000 THB/month revenue** and 5 staff members.

### Before POS System

| Item | Cost/Month |
|---|---|
| Wrong orders avg. 3 times/day x 50 THB | 4,500 THB |
| Billing errors avg. 2% of revenue | 3,000 THB |
| Wasted ingredients from poor inventory (5%) | 7,500 THB |
| Paper/stationery | 500 THB |
| **Total losses** | **15,500 THB** |

### After POS System

| Item | Cost/Month |
|---|---|
| POS system cost (average) | 1,500 - 3,000 THB |
| Wrong orders reduced 90% | Saves 4,050 THB |
| Billing errors reduced 95% | Saves 2,850 THB |
| Ingredient waste reduced 60% | Saves 4,500 THB |
| **Net savings** | **8,400 - 9,900 THB/month** |

> **Bottom line:** Invest 1,500-3,000 THB/month in a POS system, save **8,400-9,900 THB/month**. It pays for itself from month one.

## How to Choose the Right POS System

| Restaurant Size | Must-Have Features | Budget/Month |
|---|---|---|
| **Small** (1-2 tables, takeaway) | Take orders, bill, print receipts | 500 - 1,500 THB |
| **Medium** (10-30 tables) | + Table management, inventory, reports | 1,500 - 3,000 THB |
| **Large** (30+ tables, multiple branches) | + Multi-branch, staff management, CRM | 3,000 - 10,000+ THB |

### What to Check Before Choosing

- **Ease of use?** — How quickly can staff learn it?
- **Works offline?** — Internet outages are common in restaurants; this is critical
- **Multiple payment methods?** — Cash, QR PromptPay, credit card
- **After-sales support?** — When the system breaks, how fast can you get help?
